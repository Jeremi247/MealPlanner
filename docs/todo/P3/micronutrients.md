# Micronutrients

**Ask** — show the day's potassium, sodium, calcium and magnesium against daily reference intakes, as [0010](../../decisions/0010-track-what-a-supplement-cannot-carry.md) chooses. Not in it: scoring them, vitamins, supplement advice. Fibre is its own item ([P2](../P2/fibre.md)).

**Open**

- What the supplement contains. A supplement with calcium, magnesium or fish oil takes that nutrient out of the list, and iron is tracked only if the supplement lacks it.
- On the day card only, or each meal against its share too?
- How added salt is entered, since the table cannot see it.
- Warn when the fat target drops below ~20 % of kcal (testosterone, absorption of fat-soluble vitamins; Whittaker & Wu 2021)? Note mercury on tuna (light tuna 2–3 times a week)?
- Count fruit and vegetable servings, or distinct plants a week? It needs no new data, and food-group evidence is stronger than single nutrients (Aune 2017, lowest risk at ~800 g a day).
- Check the citations in 0010 against the papers.
